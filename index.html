On-Page SEO Basics: A Beginner's Guide

On-page SEO refers to everything you can optimize directly on a webpage to help it rank better and communicate clearly with both search engines and users.

What is a Title Tag?

A title tag is the clickable headline that appears in search engine results. It should be under 60 characters and include your primary keyword near the beginning.

What is a Meta Description?

A meta description is the short summary shown below the title tag in search results. While it doesn't directly affect rankings, it strongly influences click-through rate.

Why Header Tags Matter

Header tags (H1 through H6) create a logical structure that helps search engines and AI systems understand how your content is organized.
